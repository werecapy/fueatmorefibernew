Fiber Hacks

These are some things I like to do sometimes to add some grams of fiber to my daily diet!


  • Add soaked red lentils to pasta sauce and blend.
  • Make a vegetarian or vegan meal.
  • Pick a fiber filled food to base your meal around. (I particularly like this challenge.)
  • Drink chia seed water. Add one tablespoon of chia seeds to a water bottle, wait for 20 minutes, and they should soak up enough water to go down easy. The waiting period is mandatory.
  • Replace regular pasta with whole wheat pasta, or even fiber fortified alternatives.
  • Try new recipes that you really want to try.
  • Canned. Beans. Mash'em. Water'em. Season'em. Eat'em. The whole can. Put queso on it or store brand alfredo. I'm 90% sure it's the same shit, seasoned differently
